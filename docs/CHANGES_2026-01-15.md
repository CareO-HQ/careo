Changes on 2026-01-15

Overview
- Adjusted care home visibility rules and SaaS admin views.
- Improved care home detail data for members and teams.
- Simplified organization detail view to avoid listing members/teams there.

Backend (Convex)
- care home creation
  - Allowed multiple care homes per organization by removing the "single care home" guard.
- care home visibility
  - Owners now see all care homes they created.
  - Managers, nurses, and care assistants see only invited/assigned care homes.
  - Active care home resolution now prefers owner-created or invited care homes before a generic org default.
- care home details (SaaS admin)
  - Member list now dedupes users and aggregates their unit names.
  - Teams list and member list now fall back to organization teams/members when no units exist for a care home.
  - Team IDs are returned as strings to support both unit-based and org-based teams.

Frontend (SaaS admin)
- Organization detail page
  - Removed the organization-level member and team lists; details now focus on summary stats and care homes.
- Care home detail page
  - Shows member count, team count, and care home ID.
  - Lists teams and members with unit names.
